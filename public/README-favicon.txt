This project uses cafe-angular/public/favicon.ico as the favicon.

To replace it with a cafe-themed favicon (coffee cup icon):
1) Replace this file: cafe-angular/public/favicon.ico
   with a generated ICO (typically 16x16, 32x32, 48x48, 64x64) matching your desired design.
2) Keep cafe-angular/src/index.html link pointing to favicon.ico (already set).
3) Rebuild the Angular app.
